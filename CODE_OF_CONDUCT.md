# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Veyra to be a welcoming project for everyone. Contributors and maintainers agree to take part without harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Respect privacy. Never share someone else's wardrobe, photos, keys or personal details.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Trolling, or deliberately disrupting discussions
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

To raise a concern, open a private report through this repository's **Security** tab, or contact the maintainers through GitHub. The maintainers will decide what response, if any, is appropriate.
